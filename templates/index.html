Bridging The Quality Gap in Onion Mandis

Replacing subjective manual inspection with objective computer vision intelligence

⚠️ The Core Problem

  • ❌ Subjective Grading: Visual quality varies wildly between different procurement officers and Mandis.
  • ❌ Frequent Disputes: Farmers often dispute arbitrary price cuts for minor surface defects.
  • ❌ Lack of Audit Trail: Paper slips are easily lost, manipulated, or lack verifiable photographic proof.
  • ❌ Market Information Asymmetry: Farmers lack real-time transparent Mandi rates during batch delivery.

✨ The OnionGrade AI Solution

  • ✅ Automated Detection: YOLO11 locates every single onion in bulk batch photographs instantly.
  • ✅ Four-Class Quality AI: Deep CNN classifies Healthy, Damaged, Rotten, and Sprouted bulbs.
  • ✅ Official Grading: Standardized rules categorize onions into Grade A, URS, and Rejected counts.
  • ✅ Certified Digital PDF: Instant downloadable assessment certificates with annotated photos.

Step-by-Step Procurement Workflow

Seamless 8-step transparency from mandi arrival to digital payout verification

01

Farmer Selection

Officer searches farmer by ID/Mobile or creates new farmer profile instantly.

02

Image Capture

Batch sample image is uploaded via drag-and-drop or high-res camera feed.

03

YOLO11 Detection

Advanced computer vision model isolates all visible onions with bounding boxes.

04

Quality Classification

Each crop is analyzed for rot, sprouting, mechanical cut, or prime health.

05

Officer Review

Officer inspects interactive charts, checks confidence scores, and confirms batch.

06

Digital Certificate

ReportLab generates an official, tamper-proof quality assessment PDF report.

07

Farmer Dashboard

Report instantly appears in the farmer's personal portal in real-time.

08

PDF Download

Farmer downloads certified PDF with complete photographic audit evidence.
